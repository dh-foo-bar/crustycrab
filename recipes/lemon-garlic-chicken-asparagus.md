## Ingredients

- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 6 oz each)
- 300 g (about ⅔ lb) asparagus, trimmed
- 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
- Salt and pepper

## Steps

1. **Make the lemon mixture.** Heat the oven to 400°F on convection. Combine about 4 tsp of the oil with the lemon zest and juice, garlic, thyme, salt and pepper.
2. **Sear.** Pat the chicken dry and season well. Heat the remaining oil in a skillet over medium-high and sear 2–3 minutes per side.
3. **Start the chicken.** Put the chicken on a sheet pan, spoon over half the lemon mixture, and roast.
4. **Add the asparagus.** After 8–10 minutes, toss the asparagus with the rest of the lemon mixture on a second pan and add it to the oven.
5. **Finish.** Roast until the chicken reaches 165°F, 20–25 minutes total, and the asparagus is tender, 12–15 minutes.

## Notes

- **Stagger the pans:** asparagus cooks much faster than chicken, so it goes in partway through.
