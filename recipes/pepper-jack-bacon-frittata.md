## Ingredients

- 5 large eggs
- 2 tbsp milk or cream
- 3–4 slices bacon, chopped
- 1–2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp dried chives
- ½ cup shredded pepper jack
- Salt and pepper

## Steps

1. **Heat the oven.** Heat the oven to 375°F with a rack in the middle.
2. **Cook the bacon.** In an 8 in cast iron skillet over medium heat, cook the bacon until crisp, about 5 minutes. Pour off all but about 1 tbsp fat.
3. **Add the garlic.** Add the garlic to the bacon fat and stir 30–45 seconds, just until fragrant.
4. **Mix the eggs.** Whisk the eggs with the milk, chives, salt and pepper.
5. **Set the edges.** Pour the eggs over the bacon, scatter about two-thirds of the cheese on top, and cook undisturbed over medium-low 4–5 minutes, until the edges are set but the top is still wet.
6. **Bake.** Move the skillet to the oven for 5–6 minutes, until the center is just set; a slight jiggle is fine.
7. **Broil.** Switch to a hot broiler, scatter on the rest of the cheese, and broil about 6 in from the element for 2–3 minutes, until puffed and golden.
8. **Serve.** Rest a couple of minutes, then slide it onto a board and slice.

## Notes

- **Why the oven step:** the stovetop sets the bottom and edges, but the center needs surrounding heat to cook through before the broiler browns the top.
- **Five eggs** is right for an 8 in pan: a thick frittata with good structure.
- **Go easy on salt;** the pepper jack and bacon bring plenty.
