## Ingredients

### Potatoes

- 1 lb Yukon gold potatoes, peel on, in ½ in dice
- 2 tbsp neutral oil or bacon fat
- 1 tbsp butter
- ½ tsp kosher salt, plus black pepper
- ½ tsp smoked paprika and ¼ tsp garlic powder, optional

### Eggs

- 5–6 large eggs
- ¼ tsp kosher salt
- 1 tbsp butter
- Chives or scallions, and hot sauce, to serve

## Steps

1. **Steam the potatoes.** Heat the fat in a 12 in cast iron skillet over medium. Add the potatoes and salt and toss to coat. Cover and cook 8–10 minutes, stirring twice, until a knife slides in.
2. **Brown.** Uncover, raise the heat to medium-high and spread the potatoes in one layer. Cook 8–10 minutes, turning every 2–3 minutes, until deep golden on most sides.
3. **Start the eggs.** Once the potatoes are browning, whisk the eggs with the salt until uniform. Melt the butter in a 10 in nonstick skillet over medium-low until it foams without browning.
4. **Scramble.** Pour in the eggs and push them slowly with a spatula into large, soft curds, 2–3 minutes. Take them off while still glossy; they keep cooking on the plate.
5. **Finish the potatoes.** Toss the potatoes with the butter, paprika, garlic powder and pepper.
6. **Serve.** Plate the eggs and potatoes together with chives and hot sauce.

## Notes

- **The lid replaces a parboil:** the potatoes steam through first, so they brown without burning before the centers cook.
- **Keep the dice small.** At ¾ in or larger, the covered step takes several minutes longer.
