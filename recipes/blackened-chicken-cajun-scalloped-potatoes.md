## Ingredients

### Cajun blend

- 2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- ½–1 tsp cayenne
- ½ tsp black pepper

### Potatoes

- 1½ lb (680 g) Yukon gold potatoes, sliced ⅛ in thick
- 1 tbsp butter, plus more for the dish
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 3 scallions, whites and greens separated, sliced
- 1⅓ cups whole milk
- ⅓ cup heavy cream
- 1 tsp Cajun blend
- ¾ tsp kosher salt
- 2 oz pepper jack, grated

### Chicken

- 2 large chicken breasts (about 2 lb)
- 1 tbsp butter, melted
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- The rest of the Cajun blend
- 1 tbsp neutral oil

## Steps

1. **Start the oven.** Heat the oven to 425°F and butter an 8x8 (2-quart) baking dish. Mix the Cajun blend.
2. **Simmer the potatoes.** Melt the butter in a wide saucepan over medium and cook the garlic and scallion whites 1 minute. Add the milk, cream, 1 tsp of the blend, the salt and the potatoes. Simmer 10–12 minutes, stirring gently so the slices don't stick, until the edges are just tender and the liquid has thickened.
3. **Bake.** Pour into the dish, press level and top with the pepper jack. Bake uncovered 25–30 minutes, until deeply browned and a knife slides through easily.
4. **Prep the chicken.** Meanwhile, halve each breast horizontally into 4 even cutlets about ¾ in thick. Pat dry, brush with the melted butter and press the salt and remaining blend onto both sides.
5. **Blacken.** With about 15 minutes left on the potatoes, turn on the vent fan. Heat the oil in a cast iron skillet over medium-high until shimmering. Sear the cutlets 3–4 minutes per side without moving them, until the crust is deep mahogany.
6. **Finish in the oven.** Move the skillet to the oven for 4–8 minutes, until the chicken reads 155°F. Rest 5 minutes; it carries to about 162°F.
7. **Serve.** Rest the potatoes 10 minutes to set, then scatter the scallion greens over everything.

## Notes

- **Heat:** ½ tsp cayenne gives a solid kick; 1 tsp is properly hot. The pepper jack adds a little more.
- **Blackened, not burnt:** the crust should be a dark spice crust. Medium-high heat is enough.
- **At high altitude (around 5,000 ft):** water boils cooler, so if the slices aren't tender at the edges after 12 minutes of simmering, give them a few more. Underdone slices won't catch up in the oven.
