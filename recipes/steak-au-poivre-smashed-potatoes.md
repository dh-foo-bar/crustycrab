## Ingredients

### Steak

- 1.5 lb top sirloin or NY strip, 1–1¼ in thick (2 large or 4 small steaks)
- 1½ tbsp black peppercorns, coarsely cracked
- 1½ tsp kosher salt
- 1 tbsp neutral oil

### Pan sauce

- 2 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
- 1 shallot, minced
- ¼ cup brandy or cognac
- ½ cup beef stock (chicken stock also works)
- ½ cup heavy cream
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard

### Smashed potatoes

- 1.5 lb small Yukon gold potatoes, about 1½ in across
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp kosher salt, plus more for the cooking water
- Flaky salt, optional
- 2 tbsp chopped chives or parsley

## Steps

1. **Boil the potatoes.** Cover the potatoes with cold, well-salted water, bring to a boil and cook until a knife slides in easily, 18–22 minutes. Heat the oven to 450°F.
2. **Season the steaks.** Crack the peppercorns coarsely by pressing them under a heavy pan. Pat the steaks dry, season with the salt and press the pepper firmly into both sides. Leave them at room temperature while the potatoes cook.
3. **Smash and roast.** Drain the potatoes and let them steam dry for 2 minutes. Oil a sheet pan, set the potatoes on it and press each one flat to about ½ in. Drizzle with the olive oil, sprinkle with the salt and roast until deeply golden and crisp, 20–25 minutes.
4. **Sear the steaks.** Heat a cast iron skillet over medium-high until hot but not smoking. Add the neutral oil and sear the steaks 3–4 minutes per side, until they reach 125°F inside for medium-rare (130°F for medium). Rest on a board for 5–8 minutes.
5. **Start the sauce.** Pour off all but a thin film of fat. Over medium heat, melt 1 tbsp of the butter and cook the shallot until soft, about 1 minute.
6. **Deglaze.** Take the pan off the heat, add the brandy, then return it to the heat and simmer, scraping up the browned bits, until almost dry. Add the stock and reduce by half, about 2 minutes.
7. **Finish the sauce.** Stir in the cream and Dijon and simmer until the sauce coats a spoon, 2–3 minutes. Add any juices from the resting steaks, swirl in the remaining 1 tbsp butter and taste for salt.
8. **Serve.** Slice the steaks against the grain and spoon the sauce over. Finish the potatoes with flaky salt and chives.

## Notes

- **Medium-high, not smoking:** a pan that is too hot scorches the pepper crust and turns it bitter.
- **Brandy safety:** always add it off the heat. If it flames, let it burn out on its own.
- **No brandy:** use extra stock plus 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce.
- **Green peppercorns:** 1 tbsp brined green peppercorns, drained, can go into the sauce with the cream.
- **Steak cut:** NY strip is richer, adding about 80 kcal per serving.
- **Reheating:** warm the sauce gently and add sliced steak only for the last minute so it doesn't overcook.
- **At high altitude (around 5,000 ft):** water boils cooler, so the potatoes may need the full 22 minutes or a little longer.
