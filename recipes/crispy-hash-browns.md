## Ingredients

- 2–3 large russet potatoes (about 1.5 lb)
- 1 tsp salt
- 2–3 tbsp vegetable oil or butter
- Black pepper

## Steps

1. **Grate.** Wash and peel the potatoes, then grate them on the large holes of a box grater or with a food processor's grating disc.
2. **Squeeze out the moisture.** Pile the grated potato into a clean kitchen towel or cheesecloth, gather the corners and twist into a pouch, then squeeze hard over the sink to remove as much liquid as possible.
3. **Season.** Toss the potatoes with the salt and pepper in a bowl.
4. **Heat the pan.** Heat the oil or butter in a large cast iron or nonstick skillet over medium-high.
5. **Cook.** Spread the potatoes in an even layer and press down gently with a spatula. Don't stir. Cook undisturbed 4–5 minutes, until the bottom is golden and crisp.
6. **Flip.** Flip in sections with a spatula and cook 3–4 minutes more, until the second side is golden.

## Notes

- **Russets work best** because of their high starch.
- **Removing moisture is the key** to crispness. Don't crowd the pan; cook in batches if needed.
