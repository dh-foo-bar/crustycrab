## Ingredients

### Chicken

- 1.1 kg (2.5 lb) bone-in, skin-on split chicken breasts, 3–4 pieces
- ½ tsp kosher salt

### Miso-chili butter

- 3 tbsp white miso
- 1½ tbsp butter, softened
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp chili crisp
- 2 garlic cloves, grated
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
- 1 tsp rice vinegar

### Potatoes

- 680 g (1.5 lb) baby Yukon gold potatoes
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- Kosher salt
- 2 scallions, sliced
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

## Steps

1. **Boil the potatoes.** Heat the oven to 425°F. Cover the potatoes with cold salted water, bring to a boil and simmer 15–20 minutes, until a knife slides in easily.
2. **Prep and roast the chicken.** Meanwhile, pat the chicken dry, season with the salt and set skin-side up on one half of a sheet pan. Mix the miso-chili butter and rub half of it under the skin. Roast 20 minutes.
3. **Smash the potatoes.** Drain and steam-dry 2 minutes. Press each to about ½ in thick with the bottom of a mug, drizzle with the oil and season with salt.
4. **Glaze and add the potatoes.** Brush the remaining miso butter over the chicken skin. Lay the potatoes on the open half of the pan and swipe them through the drippings.
5. **Finish roasting.** Roast 20–25 minutes, until the chicken reads 160°F at the thickest part and the potato edges are crisp. Tent the chicken with foil if the skin darkens too fast; leave the potatoes in while the chicken rests if they need more time.
6. **Serve.** Rest the chicken 5 minutes. Spoon the pan juices over the potatoes and top with the scallions and sesame seeds.

## Notes

- **Under and over the skin:** half the butter goes under the skin to season the meat; the rest goes on top late, since miso and honey brown quickly.
- **At high altitude (around 5,000 ft):** water boils cooler, so the potatoes can take a few minutes longer to soften.
