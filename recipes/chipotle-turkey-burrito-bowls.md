## Ingredients

### Quick pickled onion

- ½ red onion, thinly sliced
- 60 ml (¼ cup) white vinegar
- 1 tsp sugar
- ½ tsp kosher salt

### Cilantro-lime rice

- 195 g (1 cup) jasmine rice, rinsed
- 400 ml (1⅔ cups) water
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- Juice of 1 lime
- ½ bunch cilantro, chopped

### Chipotle turkey

- 454 g (1 lb) ground turkey, 93% lean
- ½ red onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 chipotles in adobo, minced, plus 1 tbsp adobo sauce
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1½ tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 2 tsp neutral oil
- ¾ tsp kosher salt

### Charred corn

- 250 g (1½ cups) frozen corn
- 1 tsp neutral oil

### To finish

- 60 g (¼ cup) sour cream
- 1 lime, cut into wedges
- Hot sauce

## Steps

1. **Pickle the onion.** Stir the sliced onion with the vinegar, sugar and salt in a small bowl. Set aside, stirring now and then while you cook.
2. **Start the rice.** Combine the rinsed rice, water and salt in a small pot. Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer on low for 18 minutes. Take off the heat and rest, covered, 10 minutes.
3. **Char the corn.** Heat a large cast iron skillet over high with 1 tsp oil. Add the corn in one layer and leave it 2 minutes, then stir and cook 2–3 minutes more, until charred in spots. Tip into a bowl with a pinch of salt.
4. **Brown the turkey.** In the same skillet over medium-high, heat 2 tsp oil. Cook the diced onion 3 minutes, then add the turkey and salt. Break it up and cook 5–6 minutes, until browned with no pink.
5. **Season and simmer.** Stir in the garlic, chipotles, adobo sauce, tomato paste, cumin and oregano for 1 minute. Add the beans and 80 ml (⅓ cup) water. Simmer 3–4 minutes, until saucy but not wet.
6. **Finish the rice.** Fluff the rice with the lime juice and cilantro.
7. **Build the bowls.** Divide the rice, turkey and beans, and corn among 4 bowls. Top with pickled onion, 1 tbsp sour cream, a lime wedge and hot sauce.

## Notes

- **Heat:** 2 chipotles give a medium heat. Use 1 for mild or 3 for hot.
