## Ingredients

### Steaks

- 7 NY strip or tenderloin steaks, about 1½ in thick and similar in size
- 1 tbsp plus ½ tsp kosher salt
- 1¾ tsp coarsely ground black pepper
- 3½ tbsp avocado oil
- 5 tbsp unsalted butter
- 7 sprigs thyme
- 5 garlic cloves, smashed

### Chimichurri

- 2 cups finely chopped parsley (flat-leaf or curly)
- 2 tbsp fresh oregano leaves, finely chopped
- 5 garlic cloves, minced
- 5 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1⅓ cups olive oil
- ¾ tsp red pepper flakes
- ¾ tsp salt

### Garlic mashed potatoes

- 4.5 lb Yukon gold potatoes, peeled and quartered
- 7 garlic cloves, peeled
- 10½ tbsp unsalted butter
- 1⅓ cups heavy cream or whole milk, warmed
- 2½ tsp salt

### Asparagus

- 2.5 lb asparagus, woody ends trimmed
- 3½ tbsp olive oil
- ¾ tsp salt

## Steps

1. **Trim and dry-brine.** About 4 hours ahead, trim any silverskin and thin the fat cap to about ⅛ in. Salt the steaks and leave them uncovered on a rack in the fridge, flipping once at about 2 hours. Hold the pepper until just before cooking.
2. **Make the chimichurri.** Finely chop the parsley and oregano and combine with the garlic, vinegar, olive oil, pepper flakes and salt. Let it sit at room temperature at least 30 minutes.
3. **Temper the steaks.** Pull the steaks 30–40 minutes before grilling, pat them dry, and season with the pepper.
4. **Boil the potatoes.** Cover the potatoes and garlic with cold, well-salted water, bring to a boil and cook until fork-tender, 15–20 minutes.
5. **Cook the steaks gently.** Heat a grill to 375°F. Cook the steaks, flipping once, until they reach 115–118°F inside for medium-rare, 125–128°F for medium or 135–138°F for medium-well. Pull thinner steaks earlier.
6. **Mash.** Drain the potatoes and garlic and return them to the pot. Mash with the butter, then stir in the warm cream and salt until smooth. Keep covered.
7. **Sear.** Heat a cast iron skillet over high until just smoking. Pat the steaks dry again, add the avocado oil, and sear in 2–3 batches, 60–90 seconds per side, for a deep crust. Stand each steak on its fat edge for 30–60 seconds to render it.
8. **Baste.** Lower the heat slightly, add the butter, thyme and garlic, tilt the pan and spoon the butter over the steaks for 30–60 seconds per batch.
9. **Rest.** Move the steaks to a board and tent loosely with foil for about 7 minutes.
10. **Grill the asparagus.** While the steaks rest, toss the asparagus with the oil and salt and grill over high heat until lightly charred and tender, about 7 minutes.
11. **Serve.** Serve the steaks over the mash with the asparagus alongside and plenty of chimichurri spooned over.

## Notes

- **Dry surface, deep crust:** pat the steaks dry before the grill and again before the sear.
- **Batches:** tent the resting batch loosely so it doesn't steam while the next one sears.
- **Pepper last:** pepper added at the dry-brine stage scorches and turns bitter during the sear.
- **Doneness:** the pull temperatures are before the sear; the sear and rest carry each steak to its target.
- **Chimichurri ahead:** it can be made earlier in the day and refrigerated; take it out 30–45 minutes before serving and stir. If it's too loose, add more parsley rather than changing the oil or vinegar.
- **No pepper flakes:** use a scant ¼ tsp cayenne, a few dashes of hot sauce, or a minced fresh chile.
