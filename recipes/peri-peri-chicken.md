## Ingredients

### Chicken

- 1.1 kg (2.5 lb) bone-in, skin-on split chicken breasts, 3–4 pieces
- 120 ml (½ cup) bottled peri-peri sauce, divided
- 3 garlic cloves, grated
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Juice of ½ lime
- 1 tsp kosher salt

### Potatoes

- 680 g (1.5 lb) Yukon gold potatoes, cut into ¾ in pieces
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- ¾ tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper

### To finish

- Small handful of cilantro, chopped
- ½ lime, cut into wedges

## Steps

1. **Heat the oven.** Heat the oven to 450°F with a rack in the upper third. Line a large sheet pan with foil.
2. **Cut the chicken.** With a heavy knife or kitchen shears, cut each split breast in half crosswise through the bone. Smaller pieces cook in about 30 minutes.
3. **Season.** Stir together half the peri-peri sauce (60 ml), the garlic, smoked paprika, olive oil, lime juice and salt. Rub it all over the chicken, including under the skin.
4. **Arrange the pan.** Toss the potatoes with the olive oil, salt and pepper. Spread them on the sheet pan and set the chicken among them, skin side up, with space between pieces.
5. **Roast.** Roast 28–32 minutes, stirring the potatoes once halfway, until the skin is browned and the thickest piece reads 160°F next to the bone.
6. **Glaze and rest.** Brush the chicken with the remaining peri-peri sauce and rest 5 minutes. Toss the potatoes in the pan juices.
7. **Serve.** Carve the meat off the bone if you like, divide into 4 portions, and serve with the potatoes, cilantro and lime wedges.

## Notes

- **Thick pieces:** if the chicken isn't at 160°F by 32 minutes, take the potatoes off the pan and give the chicken 5 more minutes.
- **Nutrition:** assumes the skin is eaten. Removing it saves about 80 kcal and 9 g fat per serving.
