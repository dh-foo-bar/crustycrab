## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 570 g (1.25 lb) 93/7 ground turkey
- 1 tsp fennel seeds, lightly crushed
- ¾ tsp red pepper flakes, plus more to serve
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- 1¼ tsp kosher salt, divided
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 570 g (1.25 lb) potatoes, halved and sliced ¼ in thick
- 1.2 L (5 cups) chicken stock plus 240 ml (1 cup) water
- 1 bunch lacinato kale, stemmed and chopped (about 4 packed cups)
- 120 ml (½ cup) heavy cream
- 30 g (1 oz) Parmesan, finely grated, to serve

## Steps

1. **Season and sear the turkey.** Heat the oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high. Press the turkey into one layer and sprinkle with the fennel, pepper flakes, oregano, paprika, garlic powder, 1 tsp salt and the pepper. Sear undisturbed 4–5 minutes, until browned underneath, then break into small crumbles and cook until no pink remains.
2. **Cook the aromatics.** Add the onion and cook 5 minutes, until soft. Add the garlic and cook 1 minute.
3. **Simmer the potatoes.** Add the potatoes, stock and water. Bring to a boil, then simmer 12–15 minutes, until the potatoes are tender.
4. **Add the kale.** Stir it in and simmer 5 minutes, until wilted.
5. **Finish with cream.** Turn the heat to low and stir in the cream; warm through without boiling. Taste and add the remaining salt if needed.
6. **Serve.** Ladle about 2 cups per bowl and top with Parmesan and more pepper flakes.

## Notes

- **Sausage flavor:** fennel and chili flakes give plain turkey the taste of Italian sausage.
- **At high altitude (around 5,000 ft):** the potatoes can take a few minutes longer to soften.
