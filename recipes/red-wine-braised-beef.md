## Ingredients

### Braise

- 1.75 lb (800 g) chuck roast, trimmed, cut into 4 large pieces
- 1½ tsp kosher salt, 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 large yellow onion, diced
- 3 medium carrots, cut into 1 in chunks
- 4 garlic cloves, smashed
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tbsp flour
- 2 cups (475 ml) dry red wine, such as Côtes du Rhône or cabernet
- 1½ cups (355 ml) beef or chicken stock
- 2 bay leaves, 4 sprigs thyme
- 1 tsp red wine vinegar

### Mashed potatoes

- 1.25 lb (570 g) Yukon gold potatoes, peeled and chunked
- 1½ tbsp butter
- ⅓ cup milk
- Kosher salt

## Steps

1. **Season.** Heat the oven to 325°F. Pat the beef dry and season it with the salt and pepper.
2. **Sear.** Sear the beef in the oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat, 3–4 minutes per side, until deeply browned. Set it aside.
3. **Build the base.** Cook the onion and carrots in the same pot for 5 minutes. Add the garlic and tomato paste and cook 2 minutes until the paste darkens. Stir in the flour for 1 minute.
4. **Reduce the wine.** Add the wine, scrape up the browned bits, and boil 5 minutes to reduce by about a third.
5. **Assemble.** Add the stock, bay leaves, thyme and beef; the liquid should come about two-thirds up the meat. Bring to a simmer, cover, and move to the oven.
6. **Braise.** Braise 3–3½ hours, turning the beef once halfway, until a fork twists in the meat with no resistance.
7. **Make the mash.** In the last 40 minutes, boil the potatoes in salted water 20–25 minutes until tender, then mash with the butter and milk.
8. **Finish the sauce.** Lift out the beef, skim the fat, and simmer the sauce on the stove 5–10 minutes until it coats a spoon. Stir in the vinegar and adjust the salt.
9. **Serve.** Serve the beef and carrots over the mash with the sauce.

## Notes

- **At high altitude (around 5,000 ft):** allow 30–45 minutes more than a sea-level braise; the liquid simmers near 202°F.
- **Lower oven:** at 300°F, allow 3½–4 hours and reduce the sauce a few minutes longer.
