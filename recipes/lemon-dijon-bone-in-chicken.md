## Ingredients

### Marinade

- ¼ cup olive oil
- 3 tbsp lemon juice (about 1 lemon)
- 4 garlic cloves, smashed
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp dried oregano, or a few sprigs fresh thyme

### Chicken

- 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken breasts
- Neutral oil, for searing

## Steps

1. **Marinate.** Whisk the marinade ingredients together, coat the chicken, and marinate 30 minutes to 2 hours. Up to 30 minutes can be at room temperature; longer goes in the fridge, pulled 20 minutes before cooking.
2. **Heat the oven.** Heat the oven to 375°F on convection with a wire rack set in a sheet pan.
3. **Dry the skin.** Lift the chicken from the marinade and pat the skin bone-dry; wet skin won't sear.
4. **Sear.** Heat a thin film of oil in a cast iron or stainless skillet over medium-high. Sear skin-side down 4–5 minutes until deep golden, then flip for 1 minute.
5. **Roast.** Move the chicken to the rack skin-side up and roast 18–25 minutes, depending on size, until the thickest part near the bone reads 160°F.
6. **Rest.** Rest 8–10 minutes before cutting; carryover takes it to 165°F.

## Notes

- **Convection on a rack:** circulating air crisps the skin and cooks more evenly, and the rack keeps the underside from steaming. With a conventional oven, roast at 400°F and expect a couple of extra minutes and slightly softer skin.
- **At high altitude (around 5,000 ft):** moisture leaves faster, so don't overshoot the temperature, and tent loosely if the skin browns too quickly.
