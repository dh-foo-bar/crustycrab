## Ingredients

### Chile paste

- 3 dried guajillo chiles
- 2 dried ancho chiles
- 2 dried árbol chiles (1 for medium heat)
- 1 garlic clove

### Chili

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 900 g (2 lb) 93/7 ground turkey
- 1½ tsp kosher salt, divided, and ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 large yellow onion, 1 poblano and 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 5 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tbsp each chili powder and ground cumin
- 2 tsp dried oregano and 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 can (14.5 oz) fire-roasted diced tomatoes
- 2 cans (15 oz each) kidney beans and 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 480 ml (2 cups) chicken stock
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 15 g (2 tbsp) masa harina
- ½ lime

### To serve (per bowl)

- 2 tbsp shredded sharp cheddar, 1 tbsp plain Greek yogurt, sliced scallions, cilantro and a lime wedge

## Steps

1. **Toast the chiles.** Toast the dried chiles in a dry Dutch oven over medium until fragrant, about 30 seconds per side. Stem, seed and soak them in hot water 15–20 minutes.
2. **Blend the paste.** Blend the chiles with the garlic clove and ½ cup of the soaking liquid until smooth.
3. **Brown the turkey.** Heat the oil in the Dutch oven over medium-high. Press the turkey into one even layer, season with ½ tsp salt and the pepper, and sear undisturbed 5 minutes, until browned underneath. Break into chunks and cook 3 minutes more, until no pink remains. Move to a bowl.
4. **Soften the vegetables.** Add the onion, poblano and bell pepper with a pinch of salt and cook 6–7 minutes, until soft. Add the minced garlic and cook 1 minute.
5. **Cook the pastes and spices.** Add the tomato paste and chile paste and stir 2 minutes, until darkened. Add the chili powder, cumin, oregano and paprika and cook 30 seconds.
6. **Simmer.** Add both tomatoes, the beans, stock, soy sauce, the turkey with its juices and the remaining salt. Bring to a boil, then simmer partially covered 45 minutes, stirring occasionally.
7. **Thicken and finish.** Whisk the masa harina with ¼ cup water, stir it in and simmer 10 minutes more. Squeeze in the lime half, then taste and adjust the salt.
8. **Serve.** Ladle about 2 cups per bowl and add the toppings.

## Notes

- **Searing as a slab:** lean turkey steams and turns grainy if it's broken up right away. Letting it brown in one piece first keeps it tender and adds flavor.
- **Soy sauce and masa:** the soy adds savory depth without tasting like soy; the masa thickens and adds a light corn-tortilla flavor.
- **At high altitude (around 5,000 ft):** the chili reduces faster in dry air. Add a splash of stock if it gets thicker than you like.
