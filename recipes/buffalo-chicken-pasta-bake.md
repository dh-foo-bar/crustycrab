## Ingredients

### Chicken

- 1.5 lb bone-in, skin-on split chicken breasts (about 2)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- ¾ tsp salt, divided
- ¼ tsp black pepper

### Sauce

- 8 oz cream cheese, softened
- 1 cup whole milk, plus more to loosen
- ½ cup chicken stock
- ½ cup buffalo wing sauce
- ¼ cup ranch or blue cheese dressing
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp onion powder

### Cheese

- 1½ cups shredded mild Fontina (Fontal style) or Monterey Jack
- 1 cup shredded low-moisture mozzarella
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- ½ cup grated Parmesan, divided

### Pasta and topping

- 12 oz short pasta, such as penne, rotini or cavatappi
- ¾ cup panko bread crumbs
- 2 tbsp butter, melted
- 2 green onions, sliced

## Steps

1. **Boil the pasta.** Heat the oven to 375°F. Boil the pasta in well-salted water 2 minutes short of the package time. Drain without rinsing, toss with a little oil and set aside.
2. **Sear the chicken.** Pat the breasts dry and season with half the salt and the pepper. Sear skin-side down in the oil in a 12 in cast iron skillet over medium-high until deeply browned, about 5 minutes, without flipping.
3. **Roast.** Flip skin-side up and roast to about 155°F at the thickest part, 20–25 minutes. Rest on a board. Meanwhile, toss the panko with the butter and half the Parmesan, and separately toss the Fontina and mozzarella with the cornstarch.
4. **Deglaze.** Set the skillet over medium, minding the hot handle. Pour off excess fat, add the stock and a splash of water, and scrape up the browned bits. Bubble about 1 minute without reducing it away.
5. **Make the base.** Lower to medium-low, add the milk and whisk in the cream cheese until smooth and pourable.
6. **Season the sauce.** Off the heat, stir in the buffalo sauce, dressing, garlic powder and onion powder. Add the cornstarch-tossed cheeses and the remaining Parmesan and stir until glossy, without boiling. Salt to taste.
7. **Combine.** Shred the chicken off the bone and fold it into the sauce with the pasta. The mix should look almost too saucy; add a splash of milk if it only looks coated.
8. **Bake covered.** Level the top, cover tightly with foil and bake 15–20 minutes, until bubbling at the edges.
9. **Broil.** Uncover, scatter the Parmesan panko over the top and broil 2–4 minutes, watching closely, until golden. Rest 3–4 minutes and top with green onions.

## Notes

- **Staying moist:** pasta goes in 2 minutes undercooked, the mix goes in loose, the bake is covered, the chicken comes out at 155°F, the cheese goes in off the heat, and the panko only meets the broiler at the end.
- **Why cream cheese, not a roux:** a flour-thickened sauce keeps tightening in the oven and turns pasty as the pasta drinks it. Cream cheese stays loose.
- **Cheese swaps:** any smooth white melter works at the same amount, such as Muenster, Havarti or young provolone. Skip aged or sharp cheeses like cheddar, which can split.
