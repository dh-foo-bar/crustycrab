## Ingredients

### Sauce

- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp peanut oil
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 tbsp hot honey

### Stir fry

- 1.5 lb boneless, skinless chicken breasts, sliced thin against the grain
- 2 tbsp neutral oil, such as avocado or canola
- 1 large bell pepper, sliced
- 2 cups broccoli florets
- 1 medium carrot, sliced on a bias
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- ¾ tsp ground ginger
- 4 green onions, whites and greens sliced separately
- Cooked rice, to serve

## Steps

1. **Make the sauce.** Whisk together the soy sauce, peanut oil, rice vinegar, cornstarch and hot honey. Set aside.
2. **Sear the chicken.** Heat the neutral oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat until just smoking. Add the chicken in a single layer and cook without moving for 1–2 minutes, then stir and cook until just white throughout, 1–2 minutes more. Breast dries out fast, so don't overcook it. Move to a plate.
3. **Cook the vegetables.** In the same pan, stir fry the broccoli, carrot and bell pepper 3–4 minutes until crisp-tender, adding a splash of water if the pan gets dry.
4. **Add the aromatics.** Push the vegetables aside and add the garlic, green onion whites and ginger to the center. Cook 30 seconds, until fragrant.
5. **Sauce it.** Return the chicken, pour over the sauce and toss to coat. Cook about 1 minute, until the sauce thickens and glazes everything.
6. **Serve.** Spoon over rice and top with the green onion greens.

## Notes

- **Velveting:** for extra insurance against dry chicken, toss the sliced breast with 1 tsp cornstarch and 1 tsp soy sauce (taken from the sauce amounts) and let it sit 10 minutes before cooking.
- **Heat:** the hot honey covers both the sweetness and the heat. Add a pinch of red pepper flakes if you want more kick.
- **High heat and an uncrowded pan** make a good stir fry. With a smaller skillet, sear the chicken in two batches.
