## Ingredients

- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp instant yeast (or active dry)
- 1½ cups warm water (about 110°F, warm to the touch but not hot)

## Steps

1. **Mix.** Combine the flour, salt and yeast in a large bowl. Pour in the warm water and stir with a wooden spoon until it comes together into a rough, lumpy, sticky mass. Don't knead; just make sure no dry flour is left at the bottom of the bowl.
2. **Rest.** Cover with a towel or plastic wrap and leave at room temperature 20 minutes so the flour hydrates and the dough relaxes.
3. **Shape.** Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface. With floured hands, fold the edges toward the center a few times to build surface tension, then flip it and shape it into a round or oval loaf. Set it on parchment on a baking sheet.
4. **Second rise.** Cover loosely with a towel and let it rise 20 minutes while the oven heats to 450°F.
5. **Score and bake.** Cut one or two slashes about ¼ in deep across the top. Spray the top and sides with water for a crisper crust, then bake 25–30 minutes, until deep golden and hollow-sounding when tapped underneath.
6. **Cool.** Let it cool 5–10 minutes before slicing.

## Notes

- **Shaggy is right:** the dough should look messy and wet after mixing, not smooth.
- **Best the same day.** It keeps a day or two in a bread bag; refresh it in a 350°F oven for 10 minutes.
