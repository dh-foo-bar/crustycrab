## Ingredients

### Chicken and marinade

- 3 large chicken breasts (about 3 lb)
- 1 cup whole-milk Greek yogurt
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 6 garlic cloves, grated
- 2 tsp each ground cumin, ground coriander and paprika
- ½ tsp ground turmeric and ¼ tsp ground cinnamon
- ½–1 tsp cayenne
- 2½ tsp kosher salt and 1 tsp black pepper

### Vegetables

- 2 medium zucchini, cut lengthwise into ½ in planks
- 2 bell peppers, cut into flat panels
- 1 large red onion, cut into ½ in rounds
- 2 tbsp olive oil, ½ tsp kosher salt, ¼ tsp black pepper

### Drizzle and sauce

- Drizzle: 2 tbsp olive oil, juice of 1 lemon, 1 grated garlic clove, 2 tbsp chopped parsley
- Garlic yogurt sauce: ¾ cup whole-milk Greek yogurt, 2 grated garlic cloves, juice of ½ lemon, ¾ tsp kosher salt

## Steps

1. **Cut the chicken.** Halve each breast horizontally into 2 even cutlets about ¾ in thick, for 6 cutlets.
2. **Marinate.** Whisk the yogurt, oil, lemon zest and juice, garlic, spices, salt and pepper in a large bowl. Add the chicken, coat it well and refrigerate covered 2–8 hours.
3. **Make the sauces.** Stir the garlic yogurt sauce together. Whisk the drizzle in a separate bowl. Refrigerate both until serving.
4. **Heat the grill.** Heat the grill to medium-high, about 450°F, then clean and oil the grates. Take the chicken out of the fridge while it heats.
5. **Grill the vegetables.** Toss the vegetables with the oil, salt and pepper, keeping the onion rounds intact. Grill 8–10 minutes, turning once or twice, until charred in spots and just tender. Move them to a board.
6. **Grill the chicken.** Wipe off the excess marinade so it doesn't burn. Grill the cutlets 4–6 minutes per side with the lid closed, without moving them, until well marked and 155°F in the thickest part.
7. **Rest.** Rest the chicken 5 minutes; it carries to about 162°F.
8. **Finish.** Chop the vegetables into bite-size pieces and toss them with the lemon-garlic drizzle. Slice the chicken and serve with the vegetables and garlic yogurt sauce.

## Notes

- **Why yogurt:** it keeps lean breast juicy, even reheated. Don't marinate past 12 hours, or the surface turns mushy.
- **Heat:** ½ tsp cayenne is gentle; 1 tsp is noticeably spicy.
- **Storage:** keeps 4 days refrigerated. Store the chicken whole and the sauce separately; slice just before eating. Good over rice, in pita or on greens.
- **At high altitude (around 5,000 ft):** grills can run cooler; go by the thermometer, not the clock.
