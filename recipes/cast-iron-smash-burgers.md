## Ingredients

- 1 lb 80/20 ground beef
- 4 soft burger buns
- 4 slices American cheese
- 1 yellow onion, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 tbsp butter, for toasting the buns

## Steps

1. **Portion the beef.** Divide the beef into 4 loose balls. Don't pack them tight; a loose ball smashes flatter and crispier.
2. **Heat the pan.** Heat a cast iron skillet over medium-high with the oil until it just starts to smoke, about 3 minutes.
3. **Toast the buns.** Melt the butter in the pan and toast the cut sides of the buns until golden. Set aside.
4. **Smash.** Drop in a ball of beef and smash it flat with a sturdy spatula, pressing hard for about 10 seconds. Season with salt and pepper. Repeat, working in batches so you don't crowd the pan.
5. **Sear and flip.** Cook undisturbed until deeply browned and crispy at the edges, 2–3 minutes. Flip, top with a slice of cheese and a pile of onion, and cook 1 minute more.
6. **Build.** Stack on the toasted buns and serve right away.

## Notes

- **Loose beef and a very hot pan** are the whole technique.
- **Double stacks:** make 8 smaller smashes instead of 4 and stack two per bun.
- **Raw onion:** skip cooking the onion on the patty and add raw slices on top if you'd rather keep it fast.
