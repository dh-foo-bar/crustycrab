## Ingredients

### Filling

- 85 g (3 oz) pepper jack, shredded
- ⅓ cup jarred roasted red peppers, patted dry and chopped
- 1 chipotle in adobo, minced, plus 1 tsp adobo sauce
- 2 scallions, sliced

### Chicken

- 2 large chicken breasts, about 800 g (1.75 lb) total
- 1 tsp each smoked paprika, chili powder and ground cumin
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tbsp olive oil

### Potatoes

- 800 g (1.75 lb) potatoes, in 1 in chunks
- 1½ tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp garlic powder, ¾ tsp kosher salt

### Chipotle yogurt (optional)

- ¼ cup Greek yogurt, 1 tsp adobo sauce and a squeeze of lime

## Steps

1. **Roast the potatoes.** Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the potatoes with the oil, paprika, garlic powder and salt on a sheet pan and roast 35–40 minutes, flipping halfway.
2. **Make the filling.** Mix the pepper jack, red peppers, chipotle, adobo sauce and scallions.
3. **Stuff the chicken.** Cut a deep pocket along the thick side of each breast, stopping ½ in from the other edges. Pack in the filling and close each with 2 toothpicks.
4. **Season.** Mix the spices with the salt and rub over the outside of the chicken.
5. **Sear.** When the potatoes have roasted about 15 minutes, heat the oil in a cast iron skillet over medium-high. Sear the chicken 3 minutes per side, until browned.
6. **Roast.** Move the skillet to the oven beside the potatoes and roast 18–22 minutes, until the thickest part reads 160°F.
7. **Serve.** Rest 5 minutes, remove the toothpicks and halve each breast. Serve with the potatoes and the chipotle yogurt.

## Notes

- **Leftover chipotles** freeze well in a small bag; break off one at a time.
- **Pockets:** a sharp, thin knife and a cold breast make the cut easiest. Keep the pocket closed so the cheese stays inside.
