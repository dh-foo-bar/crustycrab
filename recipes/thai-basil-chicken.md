## Ingredients

### Chicken stir-fry

- 1.75 lb chicken breast, chopped into ¼–½ in pieces
- 8 garlic cloves
- 4–8 Thai chilies (4 = spicy, 8 = very spicy)
- ½ red onion, thinly sliced
- 1½ tbsp neutral oil
- 2 packed cups Thai basil leaves (Italian basil works)

### Sauce

- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp sugar
- 3 tbsp water

### Jasmine rice

- 1¼ cups jasmine rice, rinsed
- 2 cups water

### Smashed cucumber salad

- 1 English cucumber
- ½ tsp kosher salt
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp fish sauce
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- 1 Thai chili, thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp chopped cilantro

### To serve

- 4 eggs
- 2 tsp neutral oil

## Steps

1. **Start the rice.** Bring the rinsed rice and water to a boil, cover, and drop to low for 18 minutes. Take it off the heat and let it steam, covered, 10 minutes.
2. **Make the cucumber salad.** Smash the cucumber with the flat of a knife and break it into bite-size chunks. Toss with the salt, let it sit 10 minutes, then drain. Toss with the vinegar, sugar, fish sauce, garlic, chili and cilantro. Keep cold.
3. **Prep the stir-fry.** Chop the chicken small; it chops cleaner slightly firm from the fridge. Mince the garlic and chilies together, or pound them to a rough paste. Slice the onion, pick the basil leaves, and stir the sauce ingredients together.
4. **Fry the eggs.** Heat a 12 in cast iron over medium-high with the egg oil. Fry the eggs until the edges are brown and crisp and the yolks are still runny, 2–3 minutes. Set aside.
5. **Bloom the aromatics.** Raise the heat to high and add the 1½ tbsp oil. Add the garlic-chili mix and stir 20–30 seconds until fragrant, without browning.
6. **Cook the chicken.** Add the chicken and spread it out. Let it sit 1 minute, then stir-fry 3–4 minutes until no pink remains. Cook in two batches if the pan is crowded. Add the onion for the last minute.
7. **Sauce and basil.** Pour in the sauce and toss 1–2 minutes until it reduces to a glossy coating. Take the pan off the heat, add the basil and toss until it just wilts.
8. **Plate.** Rice, then the chicken, with a fried egg on top and cucumber salad on the side.

## Notes

- **Without the egg:** about 550 kcal, 51 g protein, 57 g carbs and 10 g fat per serving.
- **At high altitude (around 5,000 ft):** the rice uses a little more water and time than package directions.
