## Ingredients

### Chicken

- 725 g (1.6 lb) chicken breast, cut into ¾ in cubes
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp cornstarch

### Sauce

- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1½ tbsp rice vinegar
- 1½ tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 60 ml (¼ cup) chicken stock

### Stir-fry

- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 8–10 dried árbol chiles, snipped in half, most seeds shaken out
- 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns (optional)
- 4 scallions, whites in 1 in pieces, greens sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, sliced
- 1 in piece fresh ginger, minced
- 1 red bell pepper, in ¾ in pieces
- 2 celery stalks, sliced
- 50 g (⅓ cup) roasted unsalted peanuts

### Rice

- 1½ cups jasmine rice

## Steps

1. **Start the rice.** Rinse the rice and cook it with 2¼ cups water, covered, about 18 minutes. Rest 10 minutes off the heat.
2. **Marinate.** Toss the chicken with the soy sauce and cornstarch and let it sit 10 minutes. Stir the sauce ingredients together.
3. **Sear the chicken.** Heat 1 tbsp oil in a cast iron skillet or wok over high until shimmering. Spread the chicken in one layer, sear 2 minutes undisturbed, then toss until just cooked through, 2–3 minutes. Move to a plate.
4. **Bloom the chiles.** Lower the heat to medium. Add the remaining oil, the chiles and the peppercorns and stir 30–45 seconds, until the chiles darken but don't blacken.
5. **Cook the vegetables.** Add the scallion whites, garlic, ginger, bell pepper and celery and stir-fry 2 minutes.
6. **Glaze.** Return the chicken. Re-stir the sauce, pour it in and toss about 1 minute, until glossy.
7. **Finish.** Off the heat, stir in the peanuts and scallion greens. Serve over the rice.

## Notes

- **Heat:** whole chiles mostly add aroma. Break a few open in step 4 for more heat.
- **Sichuan peppercorns** add a tingling, citrusy note; the dish still works without them.
- **At high altitude (around 5,000 ft):** add 2–3 tbsp extra water to the rice and allow a few more minutes.
