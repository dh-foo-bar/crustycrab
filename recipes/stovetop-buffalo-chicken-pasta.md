## Ingredients

### Chicken

- 1.5 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast (about 2 large breasts)
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp neutral oil

### Pasta and sauce

- 8 oz cavatappi or penne
- Salt, for the pasta water
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1¼ cups whole milk
- 3 oz cream cheese, cubed
- ½ cup Frank's RedHot
- 1½ oz sharp cheddar, shredded

### Toppings

- 1 oz blue cheese crumbles, or a drizzle of ranch
- 2 celery stalks, thinly sliced
- 2 scallions, sliced

## Steps

1. **Boil the pasta.** Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta 2 minutes short of al dente. Reserve 1½ cups of pasta water, then drain.
2. **Prep the chicken.** While the water heats, halve each breast horizontally into 2 even cutlets. Pat dry and season with the salt, garlic powder and smoked paprika.
3. **Sear the chicken.** Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Sear the cutlets 3–4 minutes per side, until they reach 160°F inside; they rise to 165°F as they rest. Rest on a board for 5 minutes, then slice.
4. **Make the sauce.** Lower the heat to medium-low. Melt the butter in the same skillet and cook the garlic for 30 seconds. Add the milk and cream cheese and whisk until smooth, then stir in the Frank's. Take the pan off the heat and stir in the cheddar until melted.
5. **Finish the pasta in the sauce.** Return the pan to low heat, add the pasta and ½ cup of the pasta water, and toss for 1–2 minutes until the pasta is just tender. The sauce should look looser than you want; it thickens on the plate. Add more pasta water a splash at a time if it tightens.
6. **Serve.** Fold in the sliced chicken and its juices. Top with the blue cheese, celery and scallions.

## Notes

- **Why it stays saucy:** the chicken cooks once, the pasta finishes in the sauce, and nothing goes in the oven, where the pasta would keep absorbing liquid and the sauce would reduce and break.
- **Keep the heat low** once the cheese goes in so the sauce stays smooth.
- **Heat level:** add more Frank's at the end for a hotter sauce.
- **Reheating:** add a splash of milk or water before warming.
- **At high altitude (around 5,000 ft):** liquid evaporates faster, so keep extra pasta water on hand.
