## Ingredients

- 1 lb flank steak
- 1¼ tsp kosher salt, plus more for the pasta water
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp neutral oil, for searing
- 500 g linguine
- ⅓ cup good olive oil
- 6 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- ½ tsp red chili flakes
- 1 cup finely grated Parmesan
- ¼ cup chopped parsley
- 1 lemon

## Steps

1. **Prep the steak.** Pat the flank dry and season all over with the salt and pepper. Let it sit at room temperature while the pan heats.
2. **Sear it whole.** Heat a cast iron skillet over high with the neutral oil until very hot. Sear the whole flank 3–4 minutes per side, to 130°F for medium-rare.
3. **Rest.** Move the steak to a board and rest at least 5 minutes before cutting.
4. **Cook the pasta.** Boil the linguine in well-salted water, pulling it just before al dente. Reserve at least 1½ cups pasta water before draining.
5. **Build the aglio e olio.** Wipe the steak pan lightly, then add the olive oil and garlic over medium-low. Sizzle gently until the garlic is just pale gold, then add the chili flakes. Splash in a little pasta water to stop the cooking and lift the browned bits from the steak.
6. **Emulsify.** Add the linguine and a few splashes of pasta water and toss hard for a minute, until the oil turns into a glossy sauce that clings.
7. **Cut the steak.** Slice the rested flank thin against the grain, then dice it.
8. **Finish off the heat.** Kill the heat and fold in the steak so it warms through but stays pink. Zest the lemon over the top, add the Parmesan and parsley, then squeeze in half the lemon's juice and taste before adding more.

## Notes

- **Against the grain:** flank is lean and directional; slicing across the grain before dicing is what keeps it tender.
- **Pale gold garlic only:** browned garlic turns bitter and takes the whole dish with it.
- **Lemon late and light:** acid dulls if it boils, and half a lemon is usually enough to lift the beef without turning it into lemon pasta.
- **At high altitude (around 5,000 ft):** the pasta water boils cooler, so start tasting a minute or two past the package time.
