## Ingredients

- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (about 6 oz each)
- 450 g (1 lb) Brussels sprouts, halved
- 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 4 tsp balsamic vinegar
- 2 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 tsp honey
- Salt and pepper

## Steps

1. **Make the glaze.** Heat the oven to 400°F on convection. Whisk 1 tbsp of the oil with the balsamic, Dijon, garlic, honey, salt and pepper.
2. **Sear.** Pat the chicken dry and season with salt and pepper. Heat ½ tbsp oil in a skillet over medium-high and sear 2–3 minutes per side, until golden.
3. **Glaze and pan up.** Put the chicken on one sheet pan and brush with half the glaze. Toss the sprouts with the remaining ½ tbsp oil and half the remaining glaze and spread them cut-side down on a second pan.
4. **Roast.** Roast both pans 20–25 minutes, flipping the sprouts halfway, until the chicken reaches 165°F. Brush the chicken with the last of the glaze for the final 5 minutes.

## Notes

- **One oven:** the chicken and sprouts need about the same time at the same temperature, so both pans go in together.
- **Dry chicken sears best.** The quick sear adds a golden crust and shortens the oven time.
