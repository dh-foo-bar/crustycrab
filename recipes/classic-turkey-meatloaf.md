## Ingredients

- ½ cup warm water
- 1 packet (about 1 oz) dry onion soup mix
- 2 eggs
- ¾ cup ketchup
- 1½ cups bread crumbs, plain or Italian
- 2 lb ground turkey (90–93% lean)
- 1 can (8 oz) tomato sauce, optional

## Steps

1. **Dissolve the soup mix.** Heat the oven to 350°F. Stir the onion soup mix into the warm water until fully dissolved.
2. **Add the eggs and ketchup.** Add the eggs and ketchup to the soup mixture and stir until blended.
3. **Add the crumbs.** Stir in the bread crumbs until evenly mixed.
4. **Add the meat.** Add the turkey one pound at a time, mixing well after each addition so it is fully incorporated with everything else.
5. **Bake.** Pack the mixture into a loaf pan. If using the tomato sauce, pour it over the top. Bake 1½ hours, until cooked through.

## Notes

- **Tomato sauce topping:** pouring the can of tomato sauce over the loaf before baking adds moisture and flavor.
- **Beef version:** the same recipe works with 2 lb lean ground beef.
